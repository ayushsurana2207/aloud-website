A written answer no longer tells you who understood it.

Code, essays and problem sets can be produced without being understood. A one-on-one viva still shows the difference in a few minutes, but no one has time to run one for every student in a 400-person course.

Aloud runs that conversation for you, for every student, on the material you choose. You stay in charge of the questions and of every result.

How it works

From lecture notes to a class picture, in four steps

  1. 01Upload what you taughtLecture notes, slides, readings or code. Aloud finds the ideas students should be able to explain.About 2 minutes
  2. 02Approve a pool of questionsQuestions apply the idea to something new. A second AI pass checks every answer key; you approve.About 10 minutes
  3. 03Every student talks it throughEach student gets their own draw from the pool and explains out loud. The examiner asks a follow-up when it matters.About 15 minutes each
  4. 04See who understoodResults per topic and per student, with the words behind each one, as soon as students finish.Live
What you get

Not a grade. A picture of what your class understands.

  • Every topic, every student: understood, partly, or a gap.
  • The exact words behind each result, so you can check it.
  • Which questions the class found hardest, and the mix-ups behind them.
  • Signals worth a look, like long silences. Never automatic verdicts.
  • You can change any result with one click.
